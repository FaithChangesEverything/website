# Faith Changes Everything Website Design Standards

## Purpose

This document establishes the design, navigation, user experience, and development standards for the Faith Changes Everything website.
Its purpose is to ensure consistency throughout the entire ministry platform as new pages, features, and content are added.
These standards serve as the architectural blueprint for the project and should be referenced before creating any new page, component, or major feature.

---

## Design Philosophy

The Faith Changes Everything website should always reflect the heart of the ministry.

### Core Design Principles

- Christ-centered
- Warm and welcoming
- Hope-filled
- Peaceful
- Simple and uncluttered
- Easy to navigate
- Mobile friendly
- Professional without feeling corporate
- Encourage visitors to continue their journey
- Every page should feel inviting rather than overwhelming.

Every design decision should help visitors take one more step toward Jesus Christ.

---

## User Experience Standards

Every page should answer three questions immediately:

1. Where am I?
2. Where can I go?
3. What should I do next?

Visitors should never feel lost while navigating the website.
The website should gently guide visitors through their spiritual journey rather than expecting them to discover everything on their own.

---

## Journey to Hope Standards

### Journey Overview Pages

Every major Journey step shall have its own landing page.
Each Journey page should contain:

- Hero Banner
- Brief introduction
- Journey sidebar
- Lesson cards
- Progress indicators
- Scripture encouragement
- Previous / Next navigation

The Journey page should invite visitors to continue growing rather than simply presenting a list of links.

---

### Lesson Cards

Every lesson shall be displayed as an individual card.
Each card should contain:

- Representative image
- Lesson number
- Lesson title
- One-sentence description
- Primary action button

Initial button text:

- Begin Lesson

Completed lesson button text:

- Review Lesson

Future versions may include:

- Completion date
- Progress indicator
- Resume lesson

---

### Lesson Pages

Every lesson page should follow the same layout to provide a familiar experience throughout the Journey.

#### Breadcrumb Navigation

Every lesson should display breadcrumb navigation near the top of the page.
Example:

Journey to Hope → Step 4 – Grow My Faith → 4.c – The Importance of Reading God's Word

Purpose:

- Shows the visitor's current location.
- Allows quick navigation back to the parent step.
- Improves usability.

---

#### Previous / Next Navigation

Every lesson should conclude with navigation buttons.
Example:

← Back to Step Overview

Continue to Next Lesson →

Purpose:

- Encourages continued learning.
- Provides an easy return to the Step Overview.
- Creates a guided learning experience.

---

#### End of Lesson Panel

Every lesson should conclude with a "Your Journey Continues" section.
Example:

Your Journey Continues
Next Lesson
Lesson Title
Short lesson description
Continue →

Purpose:

Every lesson naturally leads into the next lesson.

---

## Progress Tracking Standards

Future versions of the website should include lesson completion tracking.

Completed lessons should display:

- Green checkmark
- Gold accent border
- Review Lesson button

Returning visitors should be able to resume where they previously stopped.

Future enhancements may include:

- Resume Journey
- Lesson history
- Dashboard
- Reading progress
- Saved favorites
- Personal notes

---

## Navigation Standards

The website should always provide multiple navigation paths.

Preferred navigation flow:

Header Navigation

↓

Journey Landing Page

↓

Lesson Page

↓

Next Lesson

↓

Return to Journey Page

↓

Continue Journey

Visitors should never reach a dead end.

Navigation should always make it obvious how to continue or return.

---

## Component Standards

The website should be built using reusable components whenever possible.

Standard components include:

- Lesson Cards
- Scripture Cards
- Song Cards
- Sermon Cards
- Prayer Cards
- Video Cards
- Pastor Letter Component
- Scripture Quote Component
- Call-to-Action Panels

Reusable components ensure visual consistency and simplify future development.

---

## Visual Standards

The overall appearance of the website should communicate:

- Hope
- Peace
- Grace
- Light
- Growth
- Authenticity

Avoid:

- Clutter
- Overly busy layouts
- Distracting animations
- Harsh color combinations

Use:

- Consistent spacing
- Plenty of white space
- Warm imagery
- Professional typography
- Consistent button styles
- Soft transitions

Every page should immediately feel like part of the Faith Changes Everything ministry.

---

## Documentation Standards

Before creating any new page or feature:

1. Review the Roadmap.
2. Review this Design Standards document.
3. Follow existing component standards whenever possible.
4. Update this document whenever a new design decision becomes a permanent standard.

The objective is to maintain a consistent user experience throughout the ministry website as it continues to grow.

---

## Future Standards

This document is intended to evolve throughout the life of the project.

Whenever a design decision affects multiple pages or establishes a new standard, it should be documented here before implementation.

Examples include:

- New reusable components
- Navigation improvements
- Accessibility standards
- Mobile design standards
- Animation standards
- Dashboard standards
- User account standards
- Administrative interface standards

## Standard: Continue Your Journey Component

Every major content page within the Faith Changes Everything website should conclude with a "Continue Your Journey" section. The purpose of this component is to guide visitors toward additional resources that reinforce the biblical truth they have just studied.

This section should be designed as a reusable component that can be included on Bible studies, sermons, devotionals, topical studies, and other ministry content throughout the website.

The component should be flexible, allowing any combination of the following resources to be displayed:

➡ Continue to Step 2 – Begin with Prayer

🎵 Related Song
Just Talk to Me

🎥 Related Sermon

Explore this topic more deeply through God's Word.

⬇ Download PDF

No matter what you're facing today, we'd be honored to pray with you.
❤️ Submit a Prayer Request

Read how God has worked in the lives of others through this same biblical truth.
▶ Read Testimonies

📚 Additional Ministry Resources
Discover more music, messages, studies, and tools to help you continue growing in your relationship with Jesus Christ.

Not every page will include every resource. The component should gracefully display only the resources that are available while maintaining a consistent appearance across the website.

### Purpose

The goal of this component is not simply to keep visitors on the website. Its purpose is to encourage continued spiritual growth by connecting related resources that deepen understanding, strengthen faith, and help visitors take their next step in their walk with Jesus Christ.

This component should become a standard design element throughout the Faith Changes Everything ministry and should be considered during the development of every new piece of content.

## Stories of Hope

The Journey to Hope landing page will feature a highlighted testimony that rotates regularly to encourage visitors through real-life stories of God's faithfulness. A dedicated "Stories of Hope" page will archive approved testimonies so visitors can explore how Christ has worked in the lives of others. Testimonies may be submitted through an online form and may be published with the contributor's name or anonymously, according to their preference.
